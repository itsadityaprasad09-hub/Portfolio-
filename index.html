About

the person behind the ink

Aditya is a freelance web developer and designer based in Patna, building end-to-end products for restaurant and lounge clients alongside his own experimental projects — while studying alongside the freelance work. He favours shipping quickly and refining from what he sees on screen over long reviews of code.

Outside client work, he's drawn to Indian classical and ambient sound — raagverse, one of the plates above, is his attempt to build a listening room for it. Most of what's here started as a note at 2 AM and became something someone actually uses.
